# V8: sevenths, trained on more pop music

## Question

V7.3 knows 24 chords. Its targets keep only the third, so C:7 and C:maj7 train as C:maj and A:min7
trains as A:min. V8 asks: **with the V7.3 model and decoders unchanged and more pop data, does a richer
label set give correct sevenths without losing major/minor accuracy?**

## Summary of the results

Yes, once the decoder stops dividing by the whole training prior. On validation (three sets, never pooled):

1. **More data helps the old vocabulary.** The 24-class V7.3 model retrained with the ChoCo export
   added gains 3.0 points majmin on RWC-P val (76.85 -> 79.80 %, network alone) and 3.0 on ChoCo val;
   GuitarSet stays level (75.49 -> 75.39 %).
2. **Sevenths come at no majmin cost.** With 60 classes (maj, min, 7, maj7, min7 on 12 roots) the
   network alone scores 68.53 % sevenths on RWC-P val against 59.58 % for the 24-class model on the
   same data, with majmin 79.67 % against 79.80 %. Seeds agree within about a point.
3. **The decoder needs a softer prior.** Dividing the network's posterior by the full training prior,
   as V5 to V7 do, raises the rare 7th classes on every frame: at 1.02 s the HMM predicts 7 on 18.9 % of
   RWC-P frames (reference 6.3 %) and sevenths falls to 65.34 %. Dividing by prior^0.25 instead puts
   the HMM above the network alone on both metrics (mean of the three sets at 1.02 s: 82.18 % majmin,
   72.38 % sevenths; network alone 80.43 / 70.77 %).
4. **maj6, sus4 and dominant 9 are not learned.** Adding them (84 and 96 classes) leaves sevenths and
   majmin level, and their own recall stays near zero: maj6 0 %, dominant 9 0 %, sus4 at most 15 %.

| Test, run once (vocabulary A, seed 1, prior power 0.25), majmin / sevenths % | s | RWC-P | GuitarSet | ChoCo |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| network alone | 0.28 | 80.20 / 66.21 | 75.89 / 58.07 | 83.35 / 74.78 |
| fixed-lag Viterbi, lag 2 (p_self 0.5) | 0.46 | 80.81 / 67.41 | 76.68 / 59.04 | 83.95 / 75.62 |
| fixed-lag Viterbi, lag 4 (p_self 0.8) | 0.65 | 81.40 / 68.15 | 77.38 / 59.46 | 84.44 / 76.08 |
| fixed-lag Viterbi, lag 8 (p_self 0.98) | 1.02 | 81.91 / 68.74 | 78.05 / 59.92 | 84.83 / 76.53 |

V7.2 on the same RWC-P and GuitarSet test songs, majmin: 78.66 / 71.90 % (network alone, 0.28 s) and
80.07 / 71.32 % (0.65 s).

## Setup

Everything is V7.3's (V7.2's causal CRNN on the C2 grid from F#1, input look-ahead 2, V6's training,
V7's online decoders and Billboard change counts) except three things.

**Data.** A curated export of the ChoCo corpus (choco_corpus_prep, commit 896a9f8): 344 recordings,
18.9 h, each a WAV, a JAMS chord annotation in that recording's time and a manifest row with its tuning
in cents. Partitions: isophonics 205 (Beatles, Queen, Michael Jackson, Zweieck, Carole King), uspop2002
94, robbie-williams 36, jaah 9 (jazz). The export stays outside the repository (`data.choco_export`);
`data/choco/SOURCE.txt` records its repository and commit, and every WAV is checked against the
manifest's SHA-256. `splits/choco.json` (seed 0): 237 train, 47 val, 60 test, grouped so no release
(isophonics, robbie-williams) or artist (uspop2002) is in two splits; jaah is train only (too few
recordings to split by artist, kept for its dense 7th, maj7 and maj6 chords). RWC-P and GuitarSet
splits are unchanged. The ChoCo cache uses the manifest tuning from the first frame (cents / 100 *
bins_per_octave / 12 in librosa's units) rather than the 24-bin estimate, which wraps at +/-25 cents
and would put 26 recordings up to a semitone off their labels. RWC-P and GuitarSet keep V7.1's cache.

**Targets.** `data_v8` maps each Harte label to a quality of the vocabulary with
`mir_eval.chord.encode` (extensions such as 9 dropped unless the vocabulary has them); class =
quality * 12 + root. Labels outside the vocabulary (dim, aug, sus2, power chords, X) get the ignore
target, as N does.

| vocabulary | qualities | classes |
|---|---|---|
| majmin (V7.3) | maj, min | 24 |
| A (MIREX sevenths) | maj, min, 7, maj7, min7 | 60 |
| B | A + maj6, sus4 | 84 |
| B9 | B + dominant 9 (9, 7(#9), 7(b9)) | 96 |

Train seconds (RWC-P + ChoCo): maj 5,795 + 27,053; min 2,152 + 8,002; 7 1,123 + 4,095; maj7 1,475 +
1,129; min7 2,417 + 2,677; maj6 239 + 490; sus4 399 + 447; 9 63 + 594. GuitarSet train holds only
triads.

**Decoder.** `model_v8.ChordCRNN` is V6's causal CRNN with an n-class output layer. The decoder's prior
is the training target frequency in all 12 keys. The Billboard counts know only maj and min:
`transitions_v8.change_matrix` splits each move among the qualities of the target's family (major:
maj, 7, maj7, maj6, 9, sus4; minor: min, min7) by their share of the prior. Two new options:
`transitions.source: train` counts the moves in the training targets instead, and
`decoder.prior_power: k` divides the posterior by prior^k (renormalised) rather than by the prior.

**Metrics.** Aligned WCSR with mir_eval `majmin` (as V0 to V7.3) and `sevenths` (maj, min, 7, maj7,
min7; other reference chords are excluded), pooled per set: RWC-P, GuitarSet and ChoCo are never
pooled with each other. B's maj6 is scored as maj, so every vocabulary is scored on the same chords.

## How the evaluation avoids leakage

Same rule as V0 to V7.3: **test is scored once, never inspected.** Every choice (vocabulary, seed,
prior power, transition matrix, p_self, lag) and every finding below is from validation. The ChoCo
test split is new and was not opened before the frozen run. `scripts/v8_test.py --freeze` ran once on
the four frozen points and writes only pooled numbers (no per-song rows, no confusion matrix); it
refuses a second run.

## Findings (all from validation)

**Why ChoCo val scores higher than RWC-P val.** V7.3, which saw no ChoCo audio, already scores 81.86 %
majmin on ChoCo val against 76.85 % on RWC-P val. Three reasons, none of them leakage:
- An easier chord mix. 7, maj7 and min7 are 14 % of ChoCo val's scored time (607 + 223 + 357 of 8,467
  s) against 26 % of RWC-P val's (276 + 219 + 648 of 4,351 s); the major family is 82 % against 74 %.
  Seventh chords blur the triad (A:min7 holds C:maj6's notes), and the model is strongest on major.
- Selection. The export's offset check uses the frozen V6 as a second opinion; recordings it reads
  badly are more often flagged, then heard or left out. V7.3 shares V6's lineage, so ChoCo val leans
  towards music this model family already reads. The size of this effect is not measured.
- It holds in every partition (V7.3, majmin): uspop2002 83.91 % (15 songs), isophonics 81.66 % (26),
  robbie-williams 78.33 % (6).

So sets are compared model against model, never with each other; RWC-P val stays the harder test.

**Seventh qualities are learned, most of the error is 7 read as maj.** A seed 0, network alone, frame
recall per quality on RWC-P val: maj 89.7 %, min 65.7 %, 7 40.6 %, maj7 37.0 %, min7 53.7 % (ChoCo:
92.3, 73.0, 30.9, 44.9, 53.3 %). 51 to 65 % of the missed 7 frames go to maj. GuitarSet's train holds
no 7ths, which is why its sevenths does not move (68.06 % against 69.32 %).

**The full prior division over-predicts sevenths.** The decoders score log P(class | frame) - log
P(class), which lifts the rare classes on every frame. Predicted share of 7 on RWC-P val (A seed 0):
6.4 % for the network alone, 15.2 % with the forward filter at p_self 0.1, 18.9 % at lag 8 p_self 0.98;
the reference holds 6.3 %. Major recall falls from 89.7 to 70.1 % while 7 recall rises from 40.6 to
77.4 %. Majmin still rises (a C:7 output is right for a C:maj reference there) and sevenths falls. The
loss is the same at p_self 0.1, where the transition matrix hardly matters. Without division (k 0) the
HMM under-predicts 7 (5.1 % on RWC-P, 2.6 % on ChoCo against 7.2 %); k 0.25 sits between and is best
on both metrics.

**The transition matrix is not the cause.** Same-root quality changes (C:maj -> C:7) get only the
smoothing count in the Billboard-based matrix. Counting the moves in the training targets instead
(`transitions.source: train`; 26,220 changes, including maj -> 7 287, maj -> maj7 186, min -> min7
249) changes the scores by at most 0.15 points (A seed 0, lag 8: RWC-P 80.58 / 65.20 % against 80.62
/ 65.34 %), so V8 keeps the Billboard matrix.

**maj6, sus4 and dominant 9 are not learned.** Network-alone recall, B and B9 on val: maj6 0 % on
every set, sus4 15.2 % (RWC-P) and 0.3 % (ChoCo) for B, 4.4 % and 0.2 % for B9, dominant 9 0 %. Their
frames go to maj. With 730 s of maj6, 846 s of sus4 and 657 s of 9 in train against 32,848 s of maj,
the plain cross-entropy never picks them; their scores tie A, so V8 keeps A.

## Test

`scripts/v8_test.py --freeze` ran once on the four frozen points; only its summary numbers were read
(table in the summary). Mean of the three sets, majmin / sevenths: 79.81 / 66.35 % (0.28 s), 80.48 /
67.36 % (0.46 s), 81.07 / 67.89 % (0.65 s), 81.60 / 68.39 % (1.02 s). Each step of look-ahead adds on
every set, as on validation. On the RWC-P and GuitarSet test songs, majmin at 0.28 s is 80.20 / 75.89 %
against V7.2's 78.66 / 71.90 %, and at 0.65 s 81.40 / 77.38 % against 80.07 / 71.32 %. Test numbers sit
below validation for sevenths on GuitarSet (58.07 % against 68.64 % for seed 1 at 0.28 s); why is not
investigated, by the rule above.

## Limitations

- **One seed on test**, the better of two on validation, which flatters it; the seed gap on validation
  is up to 0.6 points sevenths at the frozen points.
- **B and B9 have one seed each.** Their null result is clear (recall near zero), but a weighted loss
  was not tried.
- **No N class.** N and every label outside the vocabulary are ignored in training and excluded from
  scoring; the system always names a chord.
- **ChoCo val is Beatles-heavy and selected with V6's help** (see the findings); RWC-P val is the
  harder set.
- **The prior power was swept on vocabulary A seed 0 only**; seed 1 was decoded at 0.5 and 0.25, and B,
  B9 and the 24-class model only at 1.
- **Licence.** RWC-P labels and audio are CC BY-NC, the jaah labels CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, the other ChoCo
  labels CC BY 4.0; the ChoCo audio is copyrighted and not included. The V8 weights are for research and
  non-commercial use only.

## What the next version should look at

- The rare qualities: a class-weighted loss or more data for maj6, sus4 and 9, measured by their recall
  on val, before adding dim, aug, sus2.
- An N class on the 60-class model.
- The live display and the play-along check with V8's labels, by ear, on songs with many 7ths.
- The 24-bin tuning estimate in the training and live features (wraps at +/-25 cents) is a separate
  open issue.

## Reproduce

```
# cache + training, each run -> results/v8/<vocab>_seed<n> (model, prior) and its val_pp0.25 scores
python scripts/run_v8.py --vocab majmin --from-run results/v7_2/c2_la2_seed0 --out results/v8/v7_3_as_is
python scripts/run_v8.py --vocab majmin --train --seed 0
python scripts/run_v8.py --vocab A --train --seed 0       # and --seed 1
python scripts/run_v8.py --vocab B --train --seed 0
python scripts/run_v8.py --vocab B9 --train --seed 0
# decoder variants on the saved models (no retraining)
python scripts/run_v8.py --vocab A --seed 0 --prior-power 1                # -> val (full prior)
python scripts/run_v8.py --vocab A --seed 0 --prior-power 1 --transitions train   # -> val_train_tau1
python scripts/run_v8.py --vocab A --seed 0 --prior-power 0.5              # -> val_pp0.5 (0, 0.25, 0.75 alike)
# test, once, over configs/v8.yaml frozen.points
python scripts/v8_test.py --freeze
```
