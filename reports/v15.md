# V15: diminished, augmented and half-diminished chords on top of V14's N network

**Answer: yes, partly.** V15 fine-tunes V14's N network (seed 0, vocabulary A + N, 61 classes) so it
also names diminished, augmented and half-diminished chords (97 classes; dim7 is trained as dim). The
recommended run, 62.5 % of each batch from the normal training songs, epoch 10, finds on validation a
third of the half-diminished seconds (33.7 % recall, 68.2 % precision) and a quarter of the augmented
(25.2 %, 21.6 %), while the 3-set mean sevenths at lag 8 (1.02 s) stays at 73.34 % against V14's 73.44 %.
Diminished is hardly learned (2.5 % recall, 6.0 % precision). tetrads, which scores the new qualities,
rises from 66.32 to 66.71 %; majmin and mirex lose half a point, all of it on GuitarSet. One seed, the
epoch picked on validation; test is not run: **majmin 78.1 % (network alone, 0.28 s) and 79.1 %
(fixed-lag Viterbi, 0.65 s), V11's numbers, not rerun.**

## Question

Since V8 the network names five qualities (maj, min, 7, maj7, min7) and, since V14, N. Diminished,
augmented, diminished seventh and half-diminished chords have no class: their frames are left out of
the loss and out of the scores, and the network calls them something else. On validation V14 seed 0
calls a C:hdim7 most often Eb:min (34 % of its frames, the three upper notes), a C:aug C:maj (44 %) and a
C:dim Ab:maj or Ab:7 (38 %, the chord the diminished triad sits on top of). V15 asks whether the network
in use can learn these chords from the few seconds the training sets hold, by training more on the
excerpts that contain them, without losing the chords it already names.

mir_eval's majmin and sevenths leave a dim, aug, dim7 or hdim7 reference out, and count an output of one
of them on a scored chord as wrong, so the new classes can only cost those two metrics. Two more metrics
judge them: tetrads (every quality exactly, C:dim against C:dim7 is wrong) and mirex (an output sharing
at least three notes with the reference is right, so C:dim for C:dim7 and C:hdim7 for C:min7 count).

## How much there is to learn from

`scripts/v15_census.py`, results/v15/census.md, train and validation annotations only. A label is read
as data_v8 reads it (bass ignored, extensions above the octave dropped). Seconds / segments / songs:

| quality | notes | RWC-P train | ChoCo train | train, all | RWC-P val | GuitarSet val | ChoCo val | val, all |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| dim | C Eb Gb | 130 / 94 / 26 | 139 / 97 / 23 | 269 s | 15 / 9 / 3 | - | 49 / 15 / 4 | 63 s |
| aug | C E G# | 85 / 53 / 13 | 193 / 115 / 24 | 278 s | 15 / 8 / 2 | - | 8 / 5 / 1 | 23 s |
| dim7 | C Eb Gb A | 15 / 8 / 5 | 121 / 123 / 16 | 136 s | - | - | 1 / 1 / 1 | 1 s |
| hdim7 | C Eb Gb Bb | 50 / 32 / 11 | 81 / 52 / 12 | 131 s | 35 / 13 / 2 | 92 / 48 / 24 | 14 / 7 / 2 | 141 s |

The four together are 814 s of the 59,899 s that vocabulary A + N trains on (1.4 %), and 228 s of
validation. GuitarSet's train songs have none of them, but its validation songs hold 92 s of
half-diminished. mir_eval reads `C:min7(b5)` as C Eb Gb G Bb (it adds the flat fifth and keeps the fifth),
12 s of ChoCo train; `C:aug(b7)` and `(3,#5,b7,b9)` are 13 s.

## Setup

V14's network, seed 0, its features (V13's 12-bin tuning cache for RWC-P and GuitarSet, V8's for ChoCo)
and its decoder (`configs/v15.yaml`, `scripts/run_v15.py`, `src/chordtracker/data_v15.py`,
`train_v15.py`, `eval_v15.py`):

- **Vocabulary A+ (97 classes).** V14's 61 classes keep their indices (0 to 59 the five qualities, 60
  N); the new classes follow N: 61 to 72 C:dim to B:dim, 73 to 84 aug, 85 to 96 hdim7. dim7 is trained
  as dim: 136 s to train on but 1 s to validate on, so its own class could not be judged
  (`vocab_plus.new` in the config gives the 109-class vocabulary with dim7 apart). `C:min7(b5)` trains as
  hdim7 and `C:aug(b7)` as aug. Under the pitch shift every root moves within its block and N stays.
- **Decode order.** The decoder, the priors, the transition matrix and the scores see the same classes
  with N moved last, so V14's N row and column, its N weight and the scoring tables apply unchanged. The
  Billboard matrix splits each change among the qualities of the target's family by their prior share;
  aug joins the major family, dim, dim7 and hdim7 the minor one (they have a minor third).
- **Extending the output layer.** Every weight of V14 seed 0 is copied. Row C:dim starts as a copy of
  row C:min, C:aug of C:maj and C:hdim7 of C:min7 (the class with the same root and third), with its bias
  lowered by the log of the new quality's share over the related one's in the train labels (-3.2, -4.8
  and -3.6). Before any update the extended network decodes exactly as V14 does on validation (73.44 %
  sevenths at lag 8, 71.40 % for the network alone).
- **Focus excerpts and replay.** Each batch of 8 excerpts of 128 frames (11.9 s) holds replay excerpts,
  V14's: the train songs tiled from a random offset, one random pitch shift each, 2,000 per epoch, and
  focus excerpts: one quality drawn uniformly, one of its runs (595 in all: dim 322, aug 181, hdim7 92),
  an excerpt placed at random around the run so the chords before and after come with it. The focus
  excerpts take the 12 pitch shifts in turn and get two augmentations: a tempo change uniform in 1 +/- 6 %
  (the cached log-CQT read at that rate, columns interpolated linearly, targets from the nearest frame)
  and a gain uniform in +/- 3 dB (V10's, inside the compression).
- **Three runs, seed 0.** 5 replay + 3 focus excerpts per batch (62.5 % replay), 4 + 4 (50 %), and 0 + 8
  (focus excerpts only, 400 batches per epoch). Adam at 1e-4, the output layer at 5e-4 (V14 trained at
  1e-3), at most 12 epochs, patience 4.
- **Choosing the epoch.** After each epoch the validation posteriors are decoded at V14 seed 0's demo
  point (fixed-lag Viterbi at lag 8, p_self 0.98, N weight 0.1, chord to N rate x0.5, prior power 0.25),
  the evidence divided by the fine-tune's own target mix. The criterion is the mean recall of the three
  new qualities minus 10 per point of 3-set mean sevenths more than 0.3 below epoch 0. The run also keeps
  the epoch with the best sevenths.

## How the evaluation avoids leakage

Every number here is from validation. The census reads train and validation annotations only, the epoch
and the run are chosen on validation, and the test split is not loaded (data_v5 and data_choco refuse
it). The three sets are scored apart and averaged, never pooled, except the per-quality recall and
precision, which pool the validation frames because each set holds too few seconds of the new chords.
V14 seed 0 is re-scored from its saved posteriors on the same features and tables; it reproduces
V14's 73.44 % exactly.

## Findings (all from validation)

1. **Half-diminished and augmented can be learned from a few hundred seconds.** The 62.5 % run names a
   third of the half-diminished seconds, two thirds of its half-diminished calls right, and a quarter of
   the augmented seconds. Recall grows epoch by epoch (hdim7 first, from epoch 3; aug from epoch 6) and
   was still rising when the runs ended.
2. **Diminished is not learned.** 2.5 to 5.2 % recall and under 13 % precision at the chosen epochs. The
   diminished triad is three notes of a dominant seventh a major third below (C Eb Gb inside Ab C Eb Gb),
   the network already says Ab:7 or Ab:maj for it, and the 64 s of validation sit in 7 songs.
3. **The old chords hold with enough replay.** At 62.5 % replay, sevenths moves -0.10 and majmin -0.50;
   at 50 % sevenths falls 0.57 and with focus excerpts only 1.16. More focus buys a little more
   new-chord recall (hdim7 34 to 42 %) at a price the criterion does not accept.
4. **The losses are on GuitarSet.** Its sevenths falls from 69.48 to 68.74 % and its mirex from 77.73 to
   76.14 %, while RWC-P gains 0.32 and ChoCo 0.12. GuitarSet's validation holds 94 s of the new chords and
   its training none; 16 of the 33 s the network calls new there are wrong, which is about the 0.74
   points it loses.
5. **The dominant 7 gains too.** 7's recall rises from 31.2 to 36.1 % and its precision from 41.6 to
   46.8 %, min's precision from 67.9 to 70.6 %. The new classes take frames that were called 7 or min
   wrongly.
6. **A short fine-tune alone helps sevenths a little.** Each run's best-sevenths epoch, before the new
   classes speak, is at 73.44 to 73.67 % (62.5 % run, epoch 1: +0.23); one seed and an epoch chosen on
   validation, so within noise.

## Limitations

- **One seed, epochs chosen on validation.** V10 measured about 0.6 points of mean sevenths between two
  seeds of the same recipe; a fine-tune from one network varies less, but the 0.10 and 0.23 point moves
  here are within that. The epoch and the run are chosen on the validation they are scored on, so the
  numbers are optimistic.
- **Few validation seconds.** aug is 23 s in 3 songs, dim 64 s in 7, hdim7 143 s with 92 of them on
  GuitarSet (no GuitarSet in its training). Recall and precision of the new qualities move by several
  points between epochs.
- **dim7 is not judged** (1 s of validation); it trains as dim, so a C:dim7 reference called C:dim is
  wrong under tetrads and right under mirex.
- **Test not run.** V11's frozen network remains the reported test result; V15 is not frozen.
- **Run 1 trained on the CPU.** A bug in run_v15 (fixed in the same branch) moved the network to the CPU
  for the 62.5 % run; runs 2 and 3 trained on the GPU. The results do not depend on the device beyond
  float rounding, only the time does (about 18 min per epoch against 3).

## What the next version should look at

- A second seed of the 62.5 % recipe, and longer training: recall was still rising at epoch 12.
- The false new-chord calls on GuitarSet: whether they are jazz voicings the labels name otherwise, and
  whether a per-quality offset in the decoder (V10's, fitted with its guards) trims them.
- Diminished as a rootless dominant: score C:dim against Ab:7 under a looser comparison, or more
  diminished training data.
- Listening: the epoch-10 network in the play-along, to hear whether the half-diminished and augmented
  calls sound right.

## Reproduce

Training must be launched from a normal terminal, where PyTorch sees the Mac's GPU
(`data/v15_logs/runs.sh` runs the three fine-tunes and the score one after another and stops if MPS is
missing).

```bash
PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/v15_census.py                                 # results/v15/census.md
PYTHONPATH=src:scripts python scripts/run_v15.py tables                     # validation tables, under a minute
PYTHONPATH=src:scripts python scripts/run_v15.py train --seed 0 --out results/v15/ft_seed0                      # ~40 min (GPU)
PYTHONPATH=src:scripts python scripts/run_v15.py train --seed 0 --replay 4 --focus 4 --out results/v15/ft_r50_seed0
PYTHONPATH=src:scripts python scripts/run_v15.py train --seed 0 --replay 0 --focus 8 --out results/v15/ft_focus_seed0
PYTHONPATH=src:scripts python scripts/run_v15.py score                      # results/v15/val_table.md
PYTHONPATH=src python -m pytest
```

The fine-tunes start from results/v14/n_seed0/model.pt and `score` reads its saved validation
posteriors (`python scripts/run_v14.py posteriors --run results/v14/n_seed0`). Where librosa's numba
cache cannot be written, set `NUMBA_CACHE_DIR` to a folder of your own (e.g. `.tmp/<branch>/numba`,
ignored by git).
