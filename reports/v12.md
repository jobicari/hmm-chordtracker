# V12: per-quality offsets and the tuning estimate on V11's network, and the listening prototype on it

## Question

V11 froze one causal network (the pool student, seed 1) that gains on V8 on validation and on test.
V12 asks three small questions around it, with no retraining:

1. **Does V11's network still under-predict the sevenths in a way a fixed per-quality offset can
   correct?** V10 found no such offset for V8; V11's network was trained differently.
2. **Were V11's features built with the tuning estimate that does not wrap at +-25 cents** (V10's
   fix of issue #11), in training and in the live chain?
3. **Does the listening prototype, moved to V11's network and its sevenths, hold up by ear**, and at
   which decoder point?

## Summary of the results

1. **The offsets are a null result again.** Fitted on one seed, they raise lag-8 mean sevenths of
   the other seed by +0.28 in one direction and lower it by 0.06 in the other; fitted on two
   validation sets, they lower the third at every point (lag 8: -4.87 on RWC-P, -2.11 on GuitarSet,
   -0.62 on ChoCo). The network does predict too few dominant sevenths (5.4 % of RWC-P validation
   frames at lag 8 against 6.3 % in the reference), but an offset that fixes that does not carry
   across seeds or sets. V11's decoding stays as frozen.
2. **The live chain uses the fixed estimate; V11's labelled training features do not.** The
   estimate is made on the semitone grid (12 bins per octave, +-50 cents) in the whole-file features,
   the streaming features and the prototype. But the cache of RWC-P and GuitarSet features V11 was
   trained on was built before the fix: all 200 songs match features made with the old 24-bin
   estimate, none the new one. 26 songs move by more than 3 cents between the two, two of them by
   about half a semitone; the median song moves 0.5 cents. ChoCo (tuning from its manifest) and the
   unlabelled pool (cached after the fix) are not affected. The cache is rebuilt at the next retrain.
3. **By ear, the longest look-ahead works best**, as on validation, and the remaining errors fit the
   training data and the vocabulary; a moving bass under a held chord often pulls the chord off
   ([reports/play_along.md](play_along.md#second-session-v11s-network-with-sevenths-v12)).

| V11 pool student, seed 1, mean sevenths on validation, % | network alone (0.28 s) | lag 8 (1.02 s) |
|---|---:|---:|
| as frozen | 71.87 | 73.15 |
| offsets fitted on seed 0 (guard a) | 72.00 | 73.43 |
| offsets fitted on the two other sets (guard b), per held-out set | RWC-P -3.90, GuitarSet -1.85, ChoCo -0.70 | RWC-P -4.87, GuitarSet -2.11, ChoCo -0.62 |

Guard (b) rows give the change on the held-out set alone, in points; the first two rows are the
unweighted mean of the three sets.

## Setup

**Network.** V11's pool student, seed 1 (`results/v11/pool_kd1_seed1`), and for the seed guard the
same recipe with seed 0 (`results/v11/pool_kd1_seed0`); their validation posteriors are saved
(`val_posteriors.npz`). Vocabulary A: maj, min, 7, maj7, min7 on 12 roots, 60 classes.

**Decoder points.** V8's: the network alone (frame argmax, 0.28 s of total look-ahead) and
fixed-lag Viterbi at lag 2, 4 and 8 (0.46, 0.65 and 1.02 s), Billboard change matrix, the decoder
dividing by the training prior raised to 0.25, and per point the p_self with the best mean sevenths
of the three validation sets.

**Scores.** Aligned WCSR with mir_eval's majmin and sevenths, per validation set (RWC-P, GuitarSet,
ChoCo) and their unweighted mean. Aligned WCSR compares the label of each frame with the chord
annotated at that frame, whatever the look-ahead it needed: a frame labelled C:7 where the
annotation says C:7 counts in both metrics; labelled C:maj, it counts for majmin (C:7 reduces to
C:maj) but not for sevenths.

## How the evaluation avoids leakage

Everything in V12 is on validation; test is not loaded or scored. The offsets are fitted on
validation, so both guards judge them on posteriors they were not fitted on: the other seed (a), and
the held-out set (b). The tuning check reads the audio and the cached features of the training and
validation songs only.

## 1. Per-quality offsets

**Method** (V10 step 2, `scripts/v10_offsets.py`, run on V11's networks by
`scripts/run_v12.py offsets`). Five log offsets, one per quality (maj fixed at 0) and shared by the
12 roots, are added to the log posterior before decoding; for example an offset of +0.5 on 7 multiplies
the posterior of every dominant seventh by e^0.5 = 1.65 before the frame is renormalised. They are
fitted by coordinate search over -1.5 to +1.0 in steps of 0.25, maximising mean sevenths of the
fitting sets at lag 8 (applied at every point) and, separately, for the network alone.

**Fitted offsets** (maj, min, 7, maj7, min7):

| fitted on | objective | offsets | mean sevenths on the fitting data, before -> after |
|---|---|---|---|
| seed 0, three sets | lag 8 | 0, +0.50, +0.50, +0.25, +0.50 | 72.96 -> 73.33 |
| seed 1, three sets | lag 8 | 0, +0.25, -0.25, -0.75, +0.25 | 73.15 -> 73.52 |
| seed 1 without RWC-P | lag 8 | 0, +0.75, +0.75, -1.50, 0 | 73.67 -> 75.03 |
| seed 1 without GuitarSet | lag 8 | 0, 0, -1.00, 0, +0.50 | 74.23 -> 74.67 |
| seed 1 without ChoCo | lag 8 | 0, +0.50, +0.50, +0.25, +0.50 | 71.58 -> 72.32 |

The two seeds disagree in sign on 7 and maj7, and the leave-one-set-out fits change from fold to
fold (7 from -1.00 to +0.75): the offsets follow the fitting data, not a bias of the network.

**Guard (a), seeds.** Mean of the three sets, majmin / sevenths %:

| seed 0 | none | 80.75 / 71.53 | 81.27 / 72.11 | 81.77 / 72.54 | 82.11 / 72.96 |
| seed 0 | fitted on seed 1 | 80.79 / 71.32 | 81.32 / 72.06 | 81.74 / 72.51 | 82.05 / 72.90 |

On seed 1 the lag-8 gain is GuitarSet alone (70.99 -> 72.60 sevenths), with RWC-P (72.13 -> 72.03)

## 2. The tuning estimate

**Where it happens.** Before the CQT, each recording's tuning is estimated on the harmonic part of
its first 10 s (`features_v7_1.start_tuning`) and the CQT's bin frequencies are shifted by it from
then on (librosa's `tuning` argument). The estimate is the offset of the pitches from the nearest
line of a grid, so it can never exceed half the grid's spacing. Before V10 it was made on the CQT's
own grid, 24 bins per octave (a line every 50 cents), so it read only -25 to +25 cents: a recording
35 cents flat sits 15 cents above the quarter tone below its notes and read as 15 cents sharp, and
its notes then fell between the semitone bins. Since V10 (`features_v7_1.tuning_in_bins`) the
estimate is made on the semitone grid (12 bins per octave, -50 to +50 cents) and converted to the
CQT's bins; its precision is the same, 1 cent instead of 0.5.

**The live chain.** `streaming_features.StreamingFeatures` calls the same `start_tuning` once 10 s of
audio have arrived, and the prototype uses `StreamingFeatures`, so the live features use the fixed
estimate. A new test streams A4 played 40 cents sharp in 1,024-sample blocks: before 10 s (A440)
its peak is one bin above A4's, after 10 s it is on A4's bin; with the old estimate (-10 cents) it
stays a bin off.

**V11's training features.** The cache stores features, not the tuning used to make them, so
`scripts/v12_tuning.py` recomputes each song's features at the old and at the new estimate and
compares both with the cached array (mean absolute difference of the log-CQT). Over the 200 RWC-P
and GuitarSet training and validation songs:

| cache matches | songs |
|---|---:|
| the old (24-bin) estimate only | 110 |
| either (the two estimates agree within float16 rounding) | 90 |
| the new (12-bin) estimate only | 0 |

The largest difference between the cache and the old-estimate features is 0.00007, float16 rounding;
where the two estimates differ, the cache is 0.0005 to 0.14 away from the new-estimate features.
The cache was written on 28 October, the fix landed on 7 November, and V10 step 3 re-cached only the
moved validation songs, in a separate folder.

26 songs move by more than 3 cents between the two estimates, 17 of them training songs. The two
largest are training songs, a guitar take read at -24.5 cents by the old estimate and +28.0 by the
new one (+52.5), and an RWC-P song at -3.5 and -50.0 (-46.5); the next is -19.0. The median song moves
0.5 cents (`results/v12/tuning/table.md` lists all 26). So V11's labelled training stage saw the old
estimate, while its pre-training on the unlabelled pool (cached on 8 November) and the live chain
use the new one. On these sets the difference is small; the fix matters most for recordings 25 to
50 cents off A440, which RWC-P and GuitarSet barely contain. Retraining is left to the next version,
whose retrain rebuilds the cache.

## 3. The listening prototype on V11's network

`scripts/play_along.py` now runs V11's frozen network at V11's four points (keys 1 to 4), with the
sevenths in the chord names and the annotation reduced to vocabulary A; the details, the listening
findings and one fix (the last chord stays shown while a frame is late) are in
[reports/play_along.md](play_along.md#second-session-v11s-network-with-sevenths-v12). A dry run on
30 s of an RWC-P validation song keeps up with real time: 2.5 ms of analysis per 21.3 ms input block
on average, with one block of 0.39 s at the 10 s tuning step.

## Limitations

- **Two seeds, three sets.** The guards are as strong as V10's and no stronger: a small offset that
  helps every seed could still hide under the gap between V11's two seeds (0.2 to 0.4 points).
- **The tuning check covers RWC-P and GuitarSet only.** ChoCo uses its manifest's tuning, which was
  not re-estimated.
- **One listening session**, by one listener, on Beatles songs; no scores come from it.

## What the next version should look at

- **The bass.** A moving bass under a held chord pulls the chord off by ear, and the labels ignore the
  bass. Training or decoding with the bass note, scored with mir_eval's inversion-aware metrics
  (majmin_inv, sevenths_inv) and a count of chord changes where only the bass moves.
- **The tuning-consistent retrain.** Rebuild the RWC-P and GuitarSet cache with the 12-bin estimate
  (the old folder kept aside) at the next retrain.
- **Beats in a causal decoder**: whether letting chord changes happen near beats, within the 1.02 s
  look-ahead, helps; V1's beat features were offline and whole-song.
