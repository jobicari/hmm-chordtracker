# V13: beats, downbeats and the bass, around V11's network

## Question

By ear, V11's network (frozen in V11, played back by the listening prototype since V12) is steadiest at
the longest look-ahead, and two things often pull its chord off: a moving bass under a held chord, and
chord changes between bar lines. V13 asks, on validation:

1. **Can the decoder use beats or downbeats?** Chord changes are cheap near a beat and dear elsewhere,
   inside today's fixed-lag Viterbi, with the network unchanged.
2. **How often does the bass pull the chord, and can a bass output stop it?** First measured against the
   bass actually played (RWC-P's aligned MIDI), then with a bass output at decode time.
3. **Does training with the played bass help**, on features rebuilt with the tuning estimate that does
   not wrap at +-25 cents (V10's fix of issue #11), which V11's labelled training data predated?

## Summary of the results

1. **Beats add nothing; true downbeats would, estimated ones only partly.** With the annotated beats the
   decoder gains +0.15 points of mean sevenths at lag 8 (seed noise); with the annotated downbeats
   +0.85, and the setting holds when it is chosen on the other validation set (+0.53, +1.08). With
   downbeats estimated by madmom (offline) +0.39: the whole gain on RWC-P, where madmom's downbeats are
   nearly exact (F-measure 0.99), a quarter of it on GuitarSet (F-measure 0.57), and the setting no
   longer transfers between sets (+0.29 one way, -0.55 the other).
2. **The bass explains a part of the extra chord changes.** At lag 8 the network changes chord 34.5
   times a minute on RWC-P validation against 27.6 in the reference; 6.3 of the changes a minute fall
   inside a held chord. Half of those (51 %) come within 0.15 s of a bass note change, against 33 % by
   chance, and two thirds of these land on a chord rooted on the new bass note: about 1 to 2 changes a
   minute are the bass's doing.
3. **A bass output does not stop them, at decode time or in training.** V10's bass head and a new head
   trained on the played bass both find the bass (the new one 78 % of the frames with a bass note), but
   making chord changes dearer when the bass moves leaves the bass-driven changes where they were and
   lowers sevenths slightly: a real chord change usually moves the bass too. Training with the played
   bass lowers sevenths by 0.33 points at lag 8 (one seed; the other stopped at its first epoch).
4. **The tuning fix changes nothing on validation.** V11 re-scored on the rebuilt features: 73.16 %
   mean sevenths at lag 8 (73.15 % before); V11's training run again on them: 73.09 % to 73.25 %.

V13 is a null result: V11's network, seed 1, stays frozen, and test is not run again.

| mean sevenths on validation, %, rebuilt features | network alone (0.28 s) | lag 8 (1.02 s) |
|---|---:|---:|
| V11 seed 0 / seed 1 (frozen) | 71.55 / 71.85 | 73.03 / 73.16 |
| control: V11's stage B on the rebuilt cache, seed 0 / seed 1 | 71.60 / 71.83 | 73.25 / 73.09 |
| played bass, seed 0 / seed 1 (seed 1 stopped at epoch 1) | 71.14 / 70.81 | 72.92 / 72.30 |
| lag 8 with annotated downbeats (V11 seed 1, RWC-P and GuitarSet only) | | +0.85 |
| lag 8 with madmom's downbeats (V11 seed 1, RWC-P and GuitarSet only) | | +0.39 |

The last two rows are changes in points against the best constant p_self on the two sets with beat
annotations, not means of three sets.

## Setup

**Network and decoders.** V11's pool student (vocabulary A: maj, min, 7, maj7, min7 on 12 roots), and
V8's four points: the network alone (frame argmax, 0.28 s of total look-ahead) and fixed-lag Viterbi at
lag 2, 4 and 8 (0.46, 0.65, 1.02 s), Billboard change matrix, prior power 0.25, per point the p_self with
the best mean sevenths of the three validation sets.

**Scores.** Aligned WCSR with mir_eval's majmin and sevenths per set (RWC-P, GuitarSet, ChoCo) and their
unweighted mean. New in V13, the inversion-aware majmin_inv and sevenths_inv (`evaluate.METRICS`): a
frame annotated C:maj/3 counts only if the label is C:maj with E in the bass, so C:maj scores for
sevenths but not for sevenths_inv. They need references that carry the bass: RWC-P, GuitarSet (whose
labels are all root position) and, of ChoCo, the 22 Beatles validation songs matched to their Isophonics
annotations in `data/beatles/`.

**Changes per minute and the bass measure.** A held chord is a run of the annotation with one
vocabulary-A chord, the bass ignored (C:maj then C:maj/3 is one held chord). A decoded change inside a
held chord is one more than 0.3 s from its ends, so a slightly late real change is not counted. It is
near the bass when the lowest note of RWC-P's aligned MIDI bass tracks changes within 0.15 s of it, and
re-rooted when the new chord's root is the new bass note. For example, a bar of C over a bass walking
C-D-E-F, decoded C, C, Em, C, has two changes inside the held chord, both near a bass change; the first
(to Em, on the E) is re-rooted.

**MIDI.** `src/chordtracker/midi.py` reads Standard MIDI Files with the standard library (tempo map,
running status); `bass_notes` merges every track with a General MIDI bass program. 90 of RWC-P's 100
songs have a bass part (RWC_P071-080 are piano or guitar arrangements); on validation the MIDI bass's
pitch class equals the annotated bass on 78.7 % of the annotated time.

## How the evaluation avoids leakage

Everything is on validation. The beat settings are chosen on the two sets with beat annotations and
also checked across them (chosen on one, judged on the other). The trained runs use V11's training
data and early stopping, and validation decides between them; test is not loaded or scored, and V13
freezes nothing new.

## 1. Beats and downbeats in the decoder

**What V1 did, and what V13 tries.** V1 averaged the features over each beat and ran the whole decoder
on the beat grid, with beats from the whole file; V7 later found frame-level decoding at least as good.
V13 keeps every frame and changes only the transition: the self-transition probability is p_on on frames
within `width` frames of a beat and p_off elsewhere (`scripts/v12_beats.py`, `beat_fixed_lag_viterbi`;
with p_on = p_off it is today's decoder). At lag 8 the label of frame t is decided 0.74 s after it, so a
causal beat tracker may confirm a beat up to about 0.7 s late.

**Beats.** RWC-P's AIST beat annotations and GuitarSet's beat_position annotations (with the position in
the bar, hence the downbeats); ChoCo's beats are not in `data/`. Estimated: librosa's `beat_track`
(offline) and madmom 0.17's downbeat tracker (RNNDownBeatProcessor + DBNDownBeatTrackingProcessor, 3 or 4
beats a bar, offline; run in a separate environment by `scripts/v13_madmom_downbeats.py`).

| V11 seed 1, lag 8, majmin / sevenths % (changes / min) | setting (width, p_on, p_off) | RWC-P | GuitarSet | mean |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| frozen | p_self 0.98 | 81.98 / 72.13 (35) | 77.94 / 70.99 (31) | 79.96 / 71.56 (32) |
| best constant | p_self 0.95 | 82.01 / 72.14 (35) | 77.97 / 71.02 (31) | 79.99 / 71.58 (32) |
| annotated beats | 1, 0.95, 0.995 | 82.12 / 72.21 (35) | 78.19 / 71.25 (31) | 80.16 / 71.73 (32) |
| annotated downbeats | 1, 0.3, 0.998 | 82.49 / 72.62 (35) | 79.39 / 72.25 (32) | 80.94 / 72.43 (33) |
| librosa beats | 0, 0.9, 0.98 | 82.00 / 72.12 (35) | 78.04 / 71.13 (31) | 80.02 / 71.62 (32) |
| madmom beats | 1, 0.95, 0.99 | 82.10 / 72.21 (35) | 78.12 / 71.18 (31) | 80.11 / 71.70 (32) |
| madmom downbeats | 1, 0.3, 0.998 | 82.48 / 72.60 (35) | 78.38 / 71.34 (32) | 80.43 / 71.97 (33) |

| madmom downbeats | 0.989 | 0.566 |

Beats come about twice a second, so "near a beat" covers almost every chord change anyway. Bar lines
are what carries information: with p_on 0.3 and p_off 0.998 a change costs little on a downbeat and a
lot anywhere else. With madmom's downbeats the gain is all there on RWC-P (+0.46 against +0.48 with the
annotation) and mostly lost on GuitarSet's short solo-guitar takes, where madmom's bar lines are wrong
about half the time; the setting chosen on GuitarSet then hurts RWC-P. And these downbeats are offline:
madmom's downbeat tracker reads the whole song, while its online mode covers beats only.

## 2. How often the bass pulls the chord

`scripts/v13_bass.py measure`, V11 seed 1 at the frozen lag 8, the 19 RWC-P validation songs with a MIDI
bass part (77.7 min; RWC_P078 has none):

| per minute | |
|---|---:|
| reference chord changes (vocabulary A, bass ignored) | 27.6 |
| decoded chord changes | 34.5 |
| decoded changes inside a held chord | 6.3 |
| of them, within 0.15 s of a bass change | 3.2 |
| of those, re-rooted on the new bass note | 2.1 |
| bass note changes inside held chords | 53.3 |

The 6.3 changes a minute inside held chords are almost all of the 6.9 extra. 51 % of them are near a
bass change, where 33 % would be by chance (the share of the time inside held chords within 0.15 s of
one), so about 18 points, roughly 1 to 2 changes a minute, follow the bass. Seen from the bass, 6 % of
its moves under a held chord are followed by a decoded change: the network ignores most of them.

The labels cannot teach the rest. Inversions mark 10.7 % of RWC-P's training time and 8.0 % of the
Beatles validation songs, and none of GuitarSet's; a bass that walks under a held chord is not
annotated at all (0.2 to 0.5 bass-only label changes a minute).

## 3. A bass head at decode time

`scripts/v13_bass.py heads`, decode only, on V10's model with root, quality and bass heads
(`data/v10_results/heads_seed0`; its bass head was trained on the labels' bass):

- **bass-hold**: the change probability (1 - p_self) of the move into frame t is multiplied by
  1 - gamma * beta_t, where beta_t = 1 - sum_k q_t(k) q_t-1(k) is the chance that two draws from
  consecutive bass posteriors q differ. For example, a bass head sure of C at t - 1 and of E at t gives
  beta_t = 1, and at gamma 0.9 a change into t costs ten times its usual probability.
- **slash**: the chord path unchanged; a frame's label takes the bass head's note (averaged over frames
  t - 8 .. t + 8, known when frame t is decided) as its bass when it is a chord tone other than the root.

| V10 heads, lag 8 (p_self 0.98) | mean majmin / sevenths | changes / min | RWC-P inside (near bass) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| gamma 0 (the chord head alone) | 81.94 / 72.46 | 32 | 6.24 (3.17) |
| gamma 0.5 | 81.92 / 72.43 | 32 | 6.15 (3.04) |
| gamma 0.9 | 81.83 / 72.35 | 31 | 6.20 (2.96) |
| gamma 0.99 | 81.75 / 72.28 | 31 | 6.15 (2.95) |

| V10 heads, lag 8, majmin_inv / sevenths_inv % | RWC-P | GuitarSet | ChoCo (Beatles) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| root position | 76.76 / 67.55 | 77.24 / 69.69 | 79.86 / 71.16 |
| slash | 77.01 / 67.83 | 76.22 / 69.14 | 80.08 / 71.42 |

Bass-hold leaves the changes near a bass move almost where they were and costs a little everywhere:
most real chord changes move the bass too, so a bass move is no sign that only the bass moved. Slash
labels gain a quarter of a point where the references have inversions and lose on GuitarSet, whose
labels have none.

## 4. The tuning-consistent retrain and the played-bass loss

**Cache.** The RWC-P and GuitarSet log-CQT cache was rebuilt with the 12-bin tuning estimate
(`scripts/v13_cache.py`; the old one kept as `data/processed/v7_2_logcqt_Fs1_24bin_tuning`). The two
songs whose estimate moved most now match features made at the new estimate (mean absolute difference
0.00006 and 0.00003, float16 rounding; 0.140 and 0.060 from the old cache).

**Runs** (`scripts/train_v13.py`, `configs/v13.yaml`), two seeds each, both from V11's stage A model of the
same seed (pre-trained on the unlabelled pool, whose cache already had the new estimate):

- **control**: V11's stage B unchanged (labelled data, chord CE + KL to the V8 ensemble).
- **played bass**: the same plus a 13-class bass head (`model_v13.ChordCRNNBass`: 12 pitch classes or
  no bass note) and 0.5 x its cross-entropy against the lowest note sounding in the MIDI bass tracks
  (`train_v13.played_bass`), on the 51 training songs that have one; all other frames are masked. The
  pitch shift moves the bass with the audio (tested: after a shift of k, the pitch the network sees in
  the input is the shifted bass target).

| run | best epoch | val frame accuracy, mean of the sets |
|---|---|---:|
| control seed 0 / seed 1 | 10 of 15 / 10 of 15 | 72.95 / 73.13 % |
| played bass seed 0 | 10 of 15 | 72.54 % |
| played bass seed 1 | 1 of 6 | 72.27 % |

sevenths, 3.17 / 2.94 / 2.97 changes a minute near a bass move.

**Reading.** The rebuilt features change nothing measurable on these sets, which are close to A440:
the control ties V11 within 0.1 to 0.2 points at every point. The played-bass head learns the bass well
(78 % of the frames with a bass note), but the chord head does not get better at ignoring it: the
bass-driven changes stay at 3.2 a minute, RWC-P loses 0.93 points of sevenths at lag 8 and the mean
0.33 (seed 0). Learning "which note is in the bass" and "which chord is this" from the same GRU state
does not separate the two, and the bass head at decode time cannot tell a bass move under a held chord
from a chord change, as with V10's head.

## Limitations

- **Played bass, one usable seed.** Seed 1 early-stopped at its first epoch (validation frame
  accuracy 72.27 %, never bettered in the next five epochs) and is effectively a one-epoch model; it
  was not re-run with a longer patience. Seed 0 trained normally and shows no gain, so a second seed
  would not change the conclusion, but the size of the loss is one seed's.
- **Little played-bass data.** Only RWC-P has the played bass (51 training songs, about 4 h); the bass
  head never sees a GuitarSet or ChoCo bass.
- **Offline downbeats.** madmom's downbeat tracker reads the whole song; a causal estimate would do
  worse. ChoCo, the largest validation set, has no beat annotations here and is not in the beat study.
- **One threshold each** for the bass measure (0.3 s from a held chord's ends, 0.15 s to a bass change).

## What the next version should look at

The project is close to a usable system: V11's network with the play-along prototype. Before more
modelling, packaging it (one install, one command, the frozen model) is the natural step. If modelling
continues:

- **Downbeats, once a causal estimate exists**: a causal downbeat tracker (e.g. BeatNet) or a joint
  decoder over chord and position in the bar, where a change is likely on beat 1. The upper bound is
  +0.85 points at lag 8, and band recordings, where downbeats are easy, keep almost all of it.
- **Chord and bass as one target** (chord x bass position) with more played-bass data, if the moving bass
  stays the main complaint by ear.

```bash
PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/train_v13.py --seed 0 --out results/v13/control_seed0
PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/train_v13.py --seed 0 --out results/v13/bass_seed0 --bass-weight 0.5
PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/v13_eval.py                                      # results/v13/eval/
PYTHONPATH=src python -m pytest
```

Seeds 1 as seed 0. Where librosa's numba cache cannot be written, set `NUMBA_CACHE_DIR` to a folder of
your own (e.g. `.tmp/<branch>/numba`, ignored by git).
