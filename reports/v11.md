# V11: distilling V8's two-seed ensemble into one causal network, with unlabelled music

## Question

In V10 the only change that beat V8 was running V8's two seeds together and averaging their
posteriors: +0.8 points of mean sevenths for the network alone on validation, at the cost of a second
network per frame. V11 asks: **can one causal network of V8's size keep that gain, if it is trained
to reproduce the ensemble?** It asks this twice:

1. on V8's labelled training audio only (19 h);
2. after pre-training on 76 h of unlabelled music, where the ensemble's posteriors are the only
   targets.

Everything else is V8's: vocabulary A (maj, min, 7, maj7, min7 on 12 roots), the causal CRNN with
input look-ahead 2, the features, the decoders and the four decoder points.

## Summary of the results

1. **On the labelled audio alone, distillation changes nothing.** The student ties V8 seed 0 for the
   network alone (70.84 % vs 70.77 % mean sevenths) and is 0.3 to 0.4 points lower once decoded.
   On these songs the ensemble's posteriors add little that the labels do not already say.
2. **With the unlabelled music it gains, on both seeds.** After pre-training on the pool and then
   training on the labelled data, the network alone gains +0.76 (seed 0) and +0.71 (seed 1) points
   of mean sevenths over V8 with the same seed. That is more than the 0.6-point seed gap on both
   seeds, the first change since V8 to do so. At lag 8 the gain shrinks to +0.57 and +0.16, which is
   within seed noise.
3. **Pre-training on the pool alone already beats V8 seed 0.** Stage A, trained on the unlabelled
   music with no label in its loss, reaches 71.05 % for the network alone and 72.88 % at lag 8
   (V8 seed 0: 70.77 % and 72.39 %).
4. **The frozen student keeps the gain on test.** Seed 1 of the pool run, frozen on validation and
   scored once, gains +0.86 to +1.22 points of mean sevenths over V8 at the four points, with mean
   majmin level (+0.06 to +0.35).

## Setup

**Teacher.** V8's two saved vocabulary-A networks (seeds 0 and 1), frozen. For each excerpt, both
networks read the same pitch-shifted input the student sees, and their posteriors are combined as
in V10's ensemble: the renormalised geometric mean, log p = mean of the two log posteriors minus its
log-sum-exp over the 60 classes, at temperature 1. The teacher is computed on the fly, in eval
mode, so its targets are exactly the ensemble's output for that input. The teachers draw no random
numbers, so the excerpts, their order, the pitch shifts and the student's dropout are V8's for the
same seed.

**Hard and soft targets.** V8 trains on a hard target per frame: the annotated class, for example
100 % C:7. The ensemble gives a soft target: a distribution over the 60 classes, for example 55 %
C:7, 30 % C:maj and 8 % E:min7. It carries which chords are close to the right one and how sure the
frame is. Soft targets need no annotation, so they exist for any audio.

**Loss** (`train_v11`, `train.kl_weight`):

    loss = CE(student, label) + kl_weight * KL(teacher || student)

- CE is V8's cross-entropy, over the frames that have a label.
- KL(teacher || student) = sum over classes of p_teacher * (log p_teacher - log p_student),
  averaged over every frame inside the song, labelled or not. It is 0 when the student's
  distribution equals the teacher's.
- kl_weight is 1.0, so both terms count equally. With kl_weight 0 the training is V8's, and a test
  checks that one epoch then gives V8's weights exactly.

**Unlabelled pool** (`scripts/v11_pool.py`). The music library in `data/unlabelled_audio/` (not
distributed). Of its 1,151 audio files:

- 965 recordings, 76.1 h, are kept, and 964 of them cached (one FLAC is damaged and does not decode).
- Excluded for leakage: every recording by an artist of a ChoCo validation or test recording (12,
  all by Sting: ChoCo's uspop2002 part is split by artist), and every title that equals or starts
  with a ChoCo validation or test title (2, among them a second copy of a validation song). RWC-P
  and GuitarSet have no artist or title to match (their songs were written or played for the data
  set).
- Also excluded: 75 duplicates (same title and duration to the second), 3 files under 10 s and one
  unreadable file. The 93 ALAC .m4a files (7.9 h, many of them copies of WAVs) are not read by the
  audio library and are left out.

The features are V8's causal log-CQT on the C2 grid, with the live-path tuning estimate after V10's
fix (`features_v7_1.causal_log_cqt`). They take 0.95 GB as float16.

**Runs.** All on V8's data (RWC-P, GuitarSet and the ChoCo export), with early stopping on mean
validation frame accuracy (patience 5, as V8).

1. **Run 1, labelled audio only:** V8's training with the KL term. Seed 0, 16 epochs (best 11),
   85 min.
2. **Run 2, pool, seed 0.** Two stages:
   - **Stage A,** pre-training on the pool with the KL term alone (`train_v11.train_pool`). Each
     epoch draws 11,062 random excerpts of 128 frames, twice the 5,531 excerpts of a labelled epoch,
     from every recording tiled from a random offset, with V8's random pitch shift. 12 epochs (best
     7), about 10 min each.
   - **Stage B,** run 1's training started from stage A's best weights (fresh optimiser). 15 epochs
     (best 10), 81 min.

   Stage B is identical to run 1, so any difference from run 1 comes from the pre-training.
3. **Run 3, pool, seed 1:** run 2 with seed 1. Stage A 16 epochs (best 11), stage B 15 (best 10).

The decoder prior is the frequency of the real training labels, as V8 (prior power 0.25).

**Scores and decoder points.** As in V8 and V10:
- **Aligned WCSR:** the reference is shifted by the system's total look-ahead.
- **majmin:** mir_eval's scoring on maj and min triads. **sevenths:** mir_eval's MIREX sevenths.
  For example, a C:7 frame read as C:maj is right in majmin and wrong in sevenths.
- **Points:** the network alone (frame argmax, 0.28 s) and fixed-lag Viterbi at lag 2, 4 and 8
  (0.46, 0.65, 1.02 s). For each run and point, p_self is the value of `configs/v8.yaml`'s grid with
  the best mean sevenths of the three validation sets (`scripts/run_v10.py points`).

## How the evaluation avoids leakage

Same rule as every version: **test is scored, never inspected.**
- **Validation only for every choice:** the loss weight, the pool set-up, which run and seed to
  freeze, and p_self per point were compared on the validation splits of RWC-P (20 songs), GuitarSet
  (60 takes) and ChoCo (47 recordings). The three sets are reported separately, and "mean" is their
  unweighted mean.
- **The pool excludes validation and test recordings** by artist and title, as listed above.
  Six pool songs are also in the training data (and three Beatles covers by other artists). That is
  not leakage: the student already trains on those recordings with labels.
- **Freeze before test:** the frozen points (`configs/v11.yaml`, `frozen`) were committed before
  test was scored. `scripts/v8_test.py --freeze` wrote only the pooled summary per set, no per-song
  rows, and refuses a second run.
- **Early stopping on validation** is V8's practice. It makes the validation scores slightly
  optimistic, equally for V8 and every V11 run.

## Findings (all from validation)

**1. On the labelled songs the soft targets repeat the labels.** The ensemble is not memorised on
its own training songs: on RWC-P train its frame accuracy is 72.3 % and on RWC-P validation
72.2 %, with the same mean top probability (0.72 and 0.73). So the soft targets on those songs are
as uncertain as on new music. Still, run 1 ends where V8 seed 0 ends (validation frame accuracy
72.16 % against 72.06 %). Where a label exists, matching the ensemble's spread around it does not
move the network to a better solution.

**2. New audio is what carries the gain.** Run 2's stage B is run 1 started from other weights, and
it ends 0.7 points of sevenths higher for the network alone. Stage A alone, whose loss never sees a
label, already beats V8 seed 0 at every point. The student learns the ensemble's behaviour from 76 h
of music it has never heard with labels, four times the labelled audio.

**3. The gain is largest where the network decides alone.** Over the two seeds, the pool adds 0.73
points for the network alone and 0.37 at lag 8. This is the pattern of V10's ensemble itself (+0.98
and +0.63 over the two-seed average): the fixed-lag Viterbi already removes much of the
frame-to-frame noise that a better network also removes. The student keeps about three quarters of
the ensemble's gain for the network alone (0.73 of 0.98) and about 60 % at lag 8 (0.37 of 0.63).

**4. GuitarSet trades majmin for sevenths.** On seed 0, GuitarSet majmin at lag 8 falls 1.25 points
while its sevenths rise 0.51. On seed 1 both rise (+0.24 and +0.12). On test the frozen seed 1 shows
the seed 0 pattern on GuitarSet (-1.3 majmin, +2.3 sevenths). A likely reason, not tested here:
GuitarSet is solo guitar while the pool is band music, and on GuitarSet the student calls sevenths
more often, so it gains the true sevenths and loses some plain triads.

## Limitations

- **Two seeds.** The network-alone gain clears the seed gap on both seeds; the lag-8 gain does not.
  A third seed would narrow the estimate.
- **One setting.** kl_weight 1.0, temperature 1, a pool epoch of twice the labelled epoch and the
  two-stage schedule were not swept. The paper the plan follows uses a weight of 0.3 in fine-tuning.
- **The pool is one library.** It is mostly rock, pop, funk and jazz band recordings, with no solo
  guitar like GuitarSet. The .m4a files were left out.
- **The teacher is causal.** The student copies two networks of its own kind. A bidirectional
  teacher, which hears the future, was planned and not trained.
- **Per-quality recall** (maj, min, 7, maj7, min7) was not broken down; the scores above pool the
  qualities.
- **Licence.** The models are trained on V8's data (research and non-commercial use only) and on a
  private music library that is not distributed.

## What the next version should look at

1. **A bidirectional teacher** (V6's design on V8's data), gated on beating the causal network
   alone by at least 1 point of sevenths on validation, then distilled on the same pool.
2. **More, and more varied, unlabelled audio:** the student gains from audio, not from labels, so a
   larger pool with solo instruments (and the .m4a files decoded) is the cheapest next lever.
3. **A third seed and a kl_weight sweep** to pin down the lag-8 gain.

## Reproduce

```bash
# V8's saved vocabulary-A runs (model.pt, prior.json) in results/v8/A_seed0 and results/v8/A_seed1,
# V10's scoring tables in results/v10/cache (python scripts/run_v10.py cache)
export PYTHONPATH=src:scripts
python scripts/train_v11.py --seed 0 --out results/v11/kd1_seed0                  # run 1
python scripts/v11_pool.py manifest                                              # pool list and exclusions
python scripts/v11_pool.py cache --jobs 8                                        # pool features
python scripts/train_v11.py --seed 0 --out results/v11/pool_kd1_seed0 --pool     # run 2 (stage A, then B)
python scripts/train_v11.py --seed 1 --out results/v11/pool_kd1_seed1 --pool     # run 3
python scripts/run_v10.py points --run results/v11/pool_kd1_seed1 --name v11_points_pool_kd1_seed1  # each run
python scripts/v8_test.py --freeze --config configs/v11.yaml --out results/v11/test   # test, once
```
