# V10: seven small changes around V8, on the decoder side and in training

## Question

V8's 60-class causal model (vocabulary A: maj, min, 7, maj7, min7 on 12 roots) is the frozen system.
Its two seeds differ by 0.6 points of mean sevenths at the 1.02 s point, and every later idea has to
beat that gap. V10 asks: **which inexpensive changes around V8 give more correct sevenths on
validation, with V8's data, features and decoder points kept?** It tries seven, each in isolation:

1. averaging the posteriors of V8's two seeds (an ensemble);
2. one log offset per chord quality, added to the posterior before decoding;
3. a tuning estimate that does not wrap at +/-25 cents (open issue #11);
4. a no-chord gate: N wherever the input level is very low;
5. early stopping on validation sevenths instead of frame accuracy;
6. extra root, quality and bass output heads during training;
7. gain, spectral tilt and noise augmentation of the training inputs.

## Summary of the results

Mostly a null result. On validation (three sets, never pooled):

1. **The ensemble is the one real gain, and a small one.** The two seeds' posteriors, averaged in the
   log domain, reach 73.32 % mean sevenths at lag 8 (seed 1: 72.99 %). For the network alone they reach
   71.95 % (seed 1: 71.16 %). That is about half the seed gap at lag 8, and it costs a second network
   per frame.
2. **Retraining changes nothing beyond seed noise.** Early stopping on sevenths picks the same epoch as
   V8. The auxiliary heads move RWC-P up 1.3 points and ChoCo down 1.0, a net +0.07. The augmentation
   loses 1.3 points on every set.
3. **Quality offsets fit the validation mix, not the model.** In-sample they add up to 0.3 points; on a
   held-out seed or set they add nothing or lose up to 6 points.
4. **The tuning fix is right but invisible on validation.** It moves 15 of 200 RWC-P and GuitarSet
   songs by more than 5 cents (up to 52.5), but only four validation takes, by at most 7.5 cents
   (+0.01 on the mean).
5. **The level gate only wins on silence.** With mir_eval's N scoring it adds 1.0 to 1.7 points on
   RWC-P and ChoCo, all of it from no-chord seconds V8 cannot label. Where the reference is a chord it
   only loses.

V8 seed 1 stays the frozen model. The tuning fix is kept (it also feeds the live path). Every other
option stays off by default, and **test was not run**: no V10 setting was frozen.

## Setup

Everything is V8's except the one change of each step. That means vocabulary A, the causal CRNN on the
C2 grid with input look-ahead 2, the training data (RWC-P, GuitarSet and the ChoCo export) and the
online decoders with the Billboard change counts. The prior power is 0.25 and the p_self grid is
`configs/v8.yaml`'s.

**Scores.** As in V8:
- **Aligned WCSR:** the reference is shifted by the system's total look-ahead.
- **majmin:** mir_eval's scoring on maj and min triads.
- **sevenths:** mir_eval's MIREX sevenths. For example, a C:7 frame read as C:maj counts as right in
  majmin and wrong in sevenths.

A new scorer (`frame_score`) stores per song the matched and scored seconds of every class at every
frame, once. Any decoded path is then scored by a lookup; a test checks that it equals
`evaluate.score`.

**Decoder points.** The network alone (frame argmax, 0.28 s) and fixed-lag Viterbi at lag 2, 4 and 8
(0.46, 0.65, 1.02 s). In V10 p_self is chosen per point and per system as the grid value with the
best mean sevenths of the three sets, so that every system gets its own best setting. For V8 this
gives V8's own choices at lag 2 (0.5) and lag 8 (0.98 for seed 1).

**Steps 1 to 4 reuse V8's saved models** (vocabulary A, seeds 0 and 1) and change only what happens
after the network:

- **Step 1, ensemble.** The two seeds' posteriors per frame, combined in two ways:
  - geometric: the renormalised exp of the mean log posterior;
  - arithmetic: the mean.
  The decoders run on the combined posterior unchanged. The two-seed average of the scores is the
  baseline that the ensemble must beat.
- **Step 2, quality offsets.** The posterior of every class of quality q is multiplied by exp(o_q).
  maj stays at 0; min, 7, maj7 and min7 are searched coordinate-wise on a grid from -1.5 to +1.0, to
  maximise mean sevenths at lag 8 (or for the network alone). Two guards against fitting the
  validation sets:
  - (a) fit on one seed, judge on the other;
  - (b) on seed 1, fit on two sets and judge on the third.
- **Step 3, tuning.** `features_v7_1.start_tuning` estimated the tuning of the first 10 s with
  `librosa.estimate_tuning` at the CQT's 24 bins per octave. That reads only -25 to +25 cents and
  wraps: a recording 35 cents flat came out 15 cents sharp, a quarter tone off the semitone grid.
  V10 estimates at 12 bins per octave (-50 to +50 cents) and converts to 24-bin units
  (`tuning_in_bins`). The live path (`streaming_features`) calls the same function, so the fix
  reaches the play-along chain too. The validation takes whose estimate moved were recomputed and
  re-scored with V8's models. The models were not retrained, since that needs the training songs'
  features recomputed as well.
- **Step 4, level gate.** After decoding, a frame is set to N where its level falls below a
  threshold. The level is the mean of its log-CQT column, log(1 + 10 C / running level): 0 in digital
  silence and relative to the song's level so far, so it uses no future audio. It is scored two ways:
  - **with N:** mir_eval's scoring, where only an N output matches a reference N second;
  - **N-excluded:** reference N seconds left out, so the gate can only lose.

  GuitarSet has no reference N.

**Steps 5 to 7 retrain vocabulary A, seed 0** with V8's recipe plus one option
(`scripts/train_v10.py`, `train_v10`). They are compared with V8 seed 0, which has the same excerpts,
order and pitch shifts for the same seed:

- **Step 5, early stopping on sevenths.** `train.early_stop: sevenths` stops on the mean validation
  sevenths frame score of the three sets instead of their mean frame accuracy (patience 5 as V8).
- **Step 6, structured outputs.** Three linear heads on the GRU's hidden state next to the 60-class
  head:
  - root, 12 classes;
  - quality, 5 classes;
  - bass, 12 classes: the lowest note of the Harte label, so C:maj/3 has bass E.

  The loss is chord CE + 0.5 * (root CE + quality CE + bass CE). The pitch shift moves root and bass
  with the chord. Decoding reads the chord head only, so the decoders are V8's. The idea is that
  shared structure (a C:7 and a C:maj share root and bass) helps the rare qualities.
- **Step 7, input augmentation.** On top of V8's pitch shift, each training excerpt gets:
  - a random gain, uniform in +/-3 dB;
  - a linear spectral tilt, uniform in +/-6 dB from the lowest CQT row to the highest;
  - Gaussian noise of standard deviation 0.05 on the log-CQT, clipped at 0.

  Gain and tilt act on C / level inside the compression: x' = log(1 + (e^x - 1) g). A separate
  random generator draws them, so the excerpts stay V8's.

All options sit in `configs/v10.yaml` / `train_v10` and default to V8's behaviour. A test checks that
the augmentation is the identity at zero, and step 5 reproduces V8 seed 0's best epoch and every
score.

## How the evaluation avoids leakage

Same rule as every version: **test is scored, never inspected.**
- **Validation only.** Every choice in V10 was compared on the validation splits of RWC-P (20
  songs), GuitarSet (60 takes) and ChoCo (47 recordings). This covers the combination rule, the
  offsets, the gate threshold, the retrain options and p_self per point. The three sets are reported
  separately, and "mean" is their unweighted mean.
- **Guards for fitted settings.** The offsets (step 2) are judged across seeds and across sets, not
  only on the data they were fitted on.
- **Early stopping on validation** (steps 5 to 7) is V8's practice. It makes the validation scores of
  the retrains slightly optimistic, equally for V8 and for every V10 run.

No V10 setting was frozen as a new system, so **test was not run**.

## Findings (all from validation)

**1. Two seeds together beat either one, mostly where the network decides alone.** The geometric
ensemble gains 0.98 points of sevenths over the two-seed average for the network alone, but 0.63 at
lag 8. The fixed-lag Viterbi already smooths out much of the frame-to-frame disagreement that the
second seed corrects. Geometric and arithmetic averaging are within 0.06 points at every point. The
ensemble also prefers a lower p_self at lag 8 (0.8, against 0.95 and 0.98 for V8's seeds): its
posteriors are less noisy and need less smoothing. The gain is real but it doubles the network's cost
per frame. For the live chain the better route is to train one network to reproduce the ensemble
(see the next steps).

**2. Per-quality offsets learn the quality mix of the sets they are fitted on.** Fitted on GuitarSet
and ChoCo, the maj7 offset goes to -1.5: GuitarSet's validation takes have no maj7 and ChoCo few.
Applied to RWC-P, the set with the most maj7 (5.0 % of its reference frames, of which V8 seed 0
already predicts only 2.6 %), sevenths falls 6.1 points. Across seeds the fitted offsets add +0.02
and -0.05. The model's quality biases are not stable enough between data sets for a global
correction to help.

**3. The tuning fix matters for training data, not for this validation.** The wrap only hits
recordings tuned more than 25 cents from A440. Among the validation takes none is that far off, and
the largest change there is 7.5 cents, which the CQT's 50-cent bins absorb (+0.01 points). Two train
songs were a wrap or near one (52.5 and 46.5 cents of change). They only affect a retrain. The fix
is kept because it is correct and the live path uses it.

**4. The level gate labels silence; it does not read chords better.** Its whole gain with N scored
comes from reference-N seconds. Where the reference is a chord it only loses: 10 to 37 false-N
seconds per set at 0.005, rising quickly with the threshold. GuitarSet (no reference N) loses 0.23
points at the lowest threshold and 2.2 at 0.05. For the play-along display, "no chord" in silence is
the right output and a threshold of 0.005 costs little. As a model improvement it is not one.

**5. Early stopping on sevenths and on frame accuracy agree.** On V8 seed 0 both criteria peak at
epoch 14. Frame accuracy on vocabulary A already rewards the right quality, so the two criteria
differ little.

**6. Auxiliary heads trade one set for another.** The structured heads help RWC-P (+1.27 sevenths at
lag 8, +1.54 for the network alone) and hurt ChoCo (-1.04 and -0.63). The mean moves +0.07 at lag 8
and +0.28 for the network alone, both well inside the 0.6-point seed gap. Mean majmin is lower at
every point (-0.13 at lag 8). The run trained four epochs longer, which may account for part of the
per-set shifts. One seed cannot separate the two.

**7. More input variety hurts at these strengths.** Augmentation lowers sevenths on every set at
every point (lag 8: RWC-P -1.55, GuitarSet -0.38, ChoCo -1.99) and stops two epochs earlier. A
likely reason, not tested separately: the causal features already divide by a running level, so the
validation inputs vary little in gain and tilt, and the noise blurs the weak chord tones that separate
7 from maj. The three augmentations were not tried one at a time.

## Limitations

- **One seed per retrain.** Steps 5 to 7 were run on seed 0 only. A gain below the 0.6-point seed
  gap could not be shown with one run, which was the plan's threshold for a second seed (+0.5 at lag
  8). Step 6 did not reach it.
- **One strength per option.** The heads weight (0.5) and the augmentation strengths (3 dB, 6 dB,
  0.05) were not swept. Weaker augmentation might sit between V8 and step 7.
- **The tuning fix is not retrained in.** V8's models were trained on features with the old
  estimate. A retrain on the corrected training features was not run because the validation
  effect is too small to measure.
- **The ensemble was not combined with the other steps** beyond the offsets of step 2, and only
  two seeds were available.
- **Licence.** All V10 models are trained on V8's data and carry its terms: research and
  non-commercial use only.

## What the next version should look at

1. **Distil the ensemble into one causal network.** Step 1 shows that two networks together are
   worth about half a seed gap at lag 8 and 0.8 to 1 point for the network alone. A single causal
   student trained to match the ensemble's posteriors would keep that at the cost of one network.
   - **A stronger teacher:** a bidirectional CRNN (V6's design), which sees the future the causal
     student cannot.
   - **More data for the student:** about 90 hours of unlabelled music are now on disk. Distillation
     needs only the teacher's posteriors, not labels.
   - **Leakage:** recordings that overlap the validation or test splits are excluded first. A check
     of artists and titles against the ChoCo, RWC-P and GuitarSet splits found one validation song
     present (three copies) and nothing from test.
2. **Retrain with the corrected tuning,** on its own or folded into the distillation run, since the
   student's training features are recomputed anyway.
3. **The no-chord gate in the play-along display** at threshold 0.005, judged by ear rather than by
   WCSR.

## Reproduce

```bash
# V8's saved vocabulary-A runs (model.pt, prior.json) in results/v8/A_seed0 and results/v8/A_seed1
export PYTHONPATH=src:scripts
python scripts/run_v10.py cache                                   # songs' scoring tables -> results/v10/cache
python scripts/run_v10.py ensemble                                # step 1 -> results/v10/step1_ensemble
python scripts/run_v10.py offsets                                 # step 2 -> results/v10/step2_offsets
python scripts/v10_tuning.py                                      # step 3, estimates -> results/v10/step3_tuning
python scripts/run_v10.py retune                                  # step 3, re-scoring -> results/v10/step3_retune
python scripts/run_v10.py gate                                    # step 4 -> results/v10/step4_gate
python scripts/train_v10.py --seed 0 --out results/v10/stop7_seed0 --early-stop sevenths            # step 5
python scripts/train_v10.py --seed 0 --out results/v10/heads_seed0 --aux-weight 0.5                 # step 6
python scripts/train_v10.py --seed 0 --out results/v10/aug_seed0 --gain-db 3 --tilt-db 6 --noise 0.05  # step 7
python scripts/run_v10.py points --run results/v10/stop7_seed0    # and heads_seed0, aug_seed0
```

Tables and per-step results are in `results/v10/<step>/` (`table.md`, `results.json`). The retrained
runs keep `history.json`, `prior.json` and `run.json` there; model weights and caches are not tracked.
